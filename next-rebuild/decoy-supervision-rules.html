Supervision, Conduct & Program Accountability

Clear supervision protects participants, people who report concerns, and the integrity of TikNation's safety work.

Reading lesson

1. Defined scope and least privilege

Each approved participant receives only the permissions needed for a clearly defined educational task. Volunteer status never grants access to private user messages, child communications, identity records, safety reports, or moderation tools by default.

2. Supervision and boundaries

All simulation activity is fictional, adult-only, and conducted in a controlled training environment. No real targets or external contact. Participants must follow written instructions, use approved channels, and stop when a supervisor directs.

3. Complaints, withdrawal, and removal

Participants may pause or withdraw without pressure. Report safety concerns, boundary violations, conflicts of interest, or misuse of access through the designated route. TikNation may suspend or remove access while reviewing concerns. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
