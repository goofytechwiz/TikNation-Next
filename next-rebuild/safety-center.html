Urgent Child Safety Escalation

If a child is in immediate danger in the United States, call 911 now. Do not wait for a TikNation response or attempt to intervene yourself.

To report suspected online child exploitation in the United States, use the official National Center for Missing & Exploited Children CyberTipline. Do not upload, forward, or circulate sexual images or videos of minors through TikNation.

Report a safety issue

Reports are private. Child-safety concerns receive priority review. Never upload or link sexual images/videos of minors, share passwords, verification codes, payment details, or government ID numbers.

Only share a secure link to the relevant post or profile. Do not upload, forward, or link explicit images or videos involving minors. Preserve any such material privately for the appropriate authorities.

If you cannot sign in because someone changed your password or email, contact TikNation Support from the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password or login codes.

Your Safety Shield

Your account protection tools.

  • Enable two-step verification in Privacy & Security.
  • Review active sessions and sign out of devices you do not recognize.
  • Change your password if you reused it or suspect it was exposed.
  • Report suspicious messages, fake accounts, or impersonation.

TikNation also watches for unusually rapid follows and follow requests, likes, repeated comments, repeated direct messages, and bursts of new posts. These signals create private review cases—not automatic bans. Message text is not stored in the behavior-detection log; only a one-way text fingerprint is used to spot repetition.

Bot and spam reports are reviewed before enforcement. A report alone never causes an automatic ban.

Founder Safety Review

Restricted to the Founder account with completed two-step verification. Staff do not get access to this queue.

Fair and child-safe review: A report is an allegation—not proof. Child-safety reports are prioritized for prompt human review, but do not automatically restrict or penalize the reported account or a child-safety advocate. Never request, download, or circulate sexual images/videos of minors. Do not restrict an account because an opinion is unpopular, controversial, mature, or because of a creator’s profession or outside links. Review context and available lawful evidence, protect uninvolved people, use the least restrictive proportionate action, and document every decision.
