Law, Authority & Accountability

TikNation is a private platform, not law enforcement. This lesson explains boundaries; it is general education, not legal advice.

Reading lesson

1. Private citizens are not investigators

A platform volunteer has no police powers, subpoena authority, warrant authority, or special permission to access private accounts. A role title, badge graphic, or training completion does not change that.

2. Why private stings are dangerous

Impersonation, baiting, arranging meetings, trespassing, threats, harassment, recording, and public accusations can create safety, privacy, and legal risks. They can also compromise legitimate investigations or increase danger to victims.

3. Accountability and truthful communication

Never claim to be law enforcement, a licensed investigator, or an official child-protection agency. Do not promise outcomes or absolute confidentiality. Follow platform rules, preserve privacy, disclose conflicts, and report mistakes promptly. Laws vary by location; seek qualified legal advice for legal questions.
