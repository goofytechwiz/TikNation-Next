What Would You Do? Decision Practice

Choose the safest next step in fictional situations. This exercise teaches boundaries, not investigative tactics.

Scenario 1

A participant sees a concerning message during a fictional training session.

Recommended response

Stop the scenario if needed, record only the fictional training result, and notify the supervisor through the training channel.

Correct: no outside contact or evidence gathering.

Scenario 2

A friend says they are being blackmailed and asks you to confront the account.

Recommended response

Listen without blame, encourage them not to pay or send more, help them reach trusted support and official reporting, and do not confront the account.

Correct: victim support and reporting, not confrontation.

Scenario 3

A participant is asked to identify the real person behind a suspicious profile.

Recommended response

Decline. Do not dox, track, or identify anyone. Escalate the request as a program-boundary violation.

Correct: no private investigation.

Scenario 4

A volunteer feels overwhelmed by content in a lesson.

Recommended response

Stop viewing, step away, and contact the supervisor or support person. Training should not require exposure to disturbing material.

Correct: participant wellbeing takes priority.

Remember

These are fictional learning exercises only. Never apply them by contacting a real person, impersonating a minor, arranging a meeting, confronting someone, or collecting private information.
