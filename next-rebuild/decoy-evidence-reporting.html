Evidence, Privacy & Responsible Reporting

Accurate, minimal, safe reporting helps the appropriate platform or authority assess a concern without creating new harm.

Reading lesson

1. Record facts, not conclusions

When permitted by platform rules and the program's procedures, note the account name, profile URL, content URL, date/time, and a concise factual description of what you personally observed. Separate direct observations from assumptions or second-hand claims.

2. Do not collect or circulate illegal material

Never request, download, save, upload, forward, screenshot, or circulate sexual images or videos involving minors. Do not ask a child to resend material. If such material appears, stop viewing, do not share it, and follow official reporting instructions.

3. Use official reporting routes

Use the platform's report function and appropriate official channels. In the United States, suspected online child sexual exploitation can be reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline. Call 911 for immediate danger. Do not conduct follow-up contact or attempt to gather more evidence.
