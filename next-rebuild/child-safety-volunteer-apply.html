Before you apply

This is not an undercover or sting program. Volunteers may not impersonate minors, bait or privately engage suspected offenders, arrange meetings, confront suspects, or investigate people across other platforms. Real undercover investigations belong to appropriately authorized professionals.

This application is for adults aged 18+. Your age confirmation is a self-attestation, not identity verification. Founder approval requires a separate adult-eligibility and safeguarding review. Do not upload or send identity documents, photos, videos, explicit material, or case evidence.

Required first step · Volunteer Orientation

Every applicant must read the orientation and pass its 10-question knowledge check (8/10 or higher). Completion is linked to your signed-in TikNation account.
