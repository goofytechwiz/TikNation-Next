Victim Support Practice

Practice compassionate, non-investigative responses to fictional disclosures. Never ask for, download, or circulate sexual images involving minors.

Scenario 1

A teenager says someone is threatening to share a private image.

Recommended response

Say: “I'm glad you told me. This isn't your fault. You don't have to handle it alone.” Encourage them to stop responding, not pay or send more, and reach a trusted safe adult and appropriate reporting service.

Support without blame; do not ask to see the image.

Scenario 2

A person says they are afraid their family will find out.

Recommended response

Listen calmly. Do not promise absolute secrecy. Explain that limited information may need to go to people who can help keep them safe.

Be honest about confidentiality and safety.

Scenario 3

A person offers to send you the image as proof.

Recommended response

Tell them not to send it. Do not open, save, screenshot, or forward sexual material involving minors. Use official reporting instructions instead.

Never request or handle illegal sexual material.

Scenario 4

A person says the suspected offender knows where they live and is coming over now.

Recommended response

Treat immediate danger seriously. Encourage them to get to a safe place with a trusted adult and call emergency services; in the U.S., call 911.

Immediate safety comes before platform procedures.

Remember

These are fictional learning exercises only. Never apply them by contacting a real person, impersonating a minor, arranging a meeting, confronting someone, or collecting private information.
