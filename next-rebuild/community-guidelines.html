TikNation is built around freedom of expression. We want people to share their creativity, opinions, interests, identities, experiences, and work without unnecessary censorship or judgment. These guidelines focus on protecting people—not policing harmless expression.

✦ Freedom first

We don't remove content simply because it is unpopular, controversial, unconventional, or a moderator personally disagrees with it.

◈ Equal treatment

Rules apply consistently to everyday users, creators, businesses, public figures, and platform leadership. Profession alone is not a reason to ban someone.

What we welcome

  • Creative work, art, fashion, music, gaming, humor, and personal expression.
  • Opinions, criticism, debate, and respectful disagreement.
  • Adult creators and performers posting non-explicit content; a person's profession alone does not disqualify them.
  • Non-explicit promotional links to creators' websites, projects, businesses, or other platforms, subject to safety and legal rules.
  • Suggestive but non-explicit expression may be reviewed in context; it is not automatically a violation solely because it is mature or provocative.
  • Content that may be uncomfortable or controversial, provided it does not cross the safety boundaries below.

Age and mature-content protections

  • TikNation’s general minimum account age is 13, subject to stricter local legal requirements.
  • Permitted mature, non-explicit content is intended for adults aged 18 or older only.
  • Mature content should be hidden by default and shown only when a user has both chosen to see it and completed an appropriate independent age-assurance process.
  • A date-of-birth declaration or a basic 13+ minimum-age check is not proof of adult eligibility.
  • Explicit pornography and explicit sexual acts remain prohibited on TikNation, regardless of age.

Adult-only content access is not currently enabled. These protections must be implemented and tested before mature content is made available.

Where freedom has boundaries

We prohibit content and conduct that seriously endangers, exploits, or violates another person's rights. This includes:

  • Sexual exploitation or sexual content involving minors, including child sexual abuse material.
  • Nonconsensual intimate imagery, revenge porn, or sexual deepfakes depicting someone without consent.
  • Human trafficking, coercion, or exploitation.
  • Threats of violence, targeted harassment, stalking, or doxxing.
  • Fraud, scams, impersonation intended to deceive, malware, or other harmful abuse.
  • Explicit pornography or explicit sexual acts. TikNation is designed for a 13+ audience; adult sexual content cannot be made available to minors.
  • Content that violates applicable law or creates a serious, credible safety risk.

Context matters

Moderation should consider context, intent, consent, and the actual content—not just keywords, a creator's name, profession, or outside links. Artistic, educational, newsworthy, or other contextual material may require human review instead of automatic punishment.

How moderation should work

  • Explain the rule and reason when content is removed or an account is restricted, where safe and practical.
  • Use proportionate responses: warning, content restriction, temporary suspension, or permanent removal depending on severity and repeated behavior.
  • Provide an appeal path for moderation decisions.
  • Urgent exploitation and nonconsensual-content reports receive priority handling.
  • Do not use popularity, verification status, or personal relationships to exempt someone from the rules.

Child Safety Advocates & Predator-Catcher Content

TikNation supports people who educate the public about online grooming, help young people recognize unsafe behavior, and responsibly report suspected exploitation. A creator's use of a fictional decoy identity does not, by itself, make child-safety awareness content a violation.

  • Responsible awareness is welcome: educational explainers, prevention advice, carefully edited investigations, and non-graphic discussion of online grooming.
  • Fictional decoys: a creator may describe or depict a clearly disclosed, fully fictional adult persona using a synthetic, adult-looking image, including a non-explicit simulated webcam scene, only as part of responsible awareness or reporting. Never use or imitate an actual child's image, identity, voice, or footage. A simulated webcam scene must not depict a child and must never be sexualized.
  • No real-child involvement: creators must not recruit, expose, direct-message, or place a real minor into a sting, confrontation, or risky investigation.
  • No explicit material: sexual images, videos, or explicit sexualized depictions of minors are prohibited, including material submitted as supposed evidence. Do not upload, repost, or circulate it; report it through the proper safety channel.
  • No vigilante targeting: no doxxing, threats, harassment campaigns, calls for followers to investigate or confront someone, or publishing private identifying information.
  • No public sting livestreams: live confrontations and real-time operations are prohibited because they can endanger people, compromise investigations, and trigger harassment.
  • Preserve context and report: do not edit or present allegations as proven facts. Keep relevant evidence secure and provide it to appropriate authorities. TikNation may restrict identifying details while a report is reviewed.

Fictional decoy imagery or simulated webcam scenes are not proof that a person committed a crime. TikNation reviews the actual conduct and context, protects uninvolved people, and does not determine criminal guilt. Responsible advocates should not be punished merely for discussing child safety or conducting lawful, non-harmful awareness work. The TikNation Child Safety Advocate designation reflects an approved platform application only; it does not certify law-enforcement status, professional qualifications, or the truth of an allegation.

Reports and appeals

If you believe content violates these guidelines, report it through TikNation's Safety Shield. If your own content or account action was handled incorrectly, you should be able to request a review. Reports are not a tool for silencing opinions or personal disputes.
