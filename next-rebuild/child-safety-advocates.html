What the designation means

Approved advocates receive a public Child Safety Advocate designation. They keep their ordinary TikNation account and creator features. The designation does not grant access to private messages, private user data, hidden search tools, account identities, or moderation controls. It does not exempt anyone from Community Guidelines.

Important boundaries
Never involve real minors in stings or use a real child's image, voice, or footage as bait. If an educational demonstration uses a decoy, it must be a clearly disclosed fictional adult persona with a synthetic, adult-looking image; any simulated webcam scene must be non-explicit and must not depict a child. No public doxxing, threats, viewer mobilization, public accusations, livestreamed confrontations, or circulation of sexual material involving minors. Report credible concerns privately to the appropriate authorities and TikNation.

Apply for the designation

Independent advocates may apply. Organization membership is optional. Keep your application focused on public work and safe practices; do not submit identity documents, private case files, names of alleged offenders, or explicit material.
