Before you begin

Every person, message, account, and event in this simulator is fictional. This is educational practice—not a live investigation, emergency service, or substitute for help from trusted people and appropriate authorities.

For the best learning experience, read the related lesson and watch the relevant trusted videos first. Then choose a scenario, read carefully, make each decision, and review the explanation. Your score is calculated in this browser only; it is not sent to TikNation or saved to an account.

Real-world help and reporting

If a situation is happening now, step away from the interaction and get help. If someone is in immediate danger in the U.S., call 911. For suspected online fraud, use official reporting channels rather than replying to the sender.

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center — report suspected internet crime.
National Center for Missing & Exploited Children — child safety help and reporting.
