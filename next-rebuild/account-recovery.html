Recover your account.

If someone took over your account, we want to help you recover your original identity—not make you start over. Your content, followers, badges, and history are protected while a recovery request is reviewed.

Request account recovery

Submit a private recovery request while signed in. Never include passwords, login codes, payment information, or government ID details.

Submitting a request does not reset your password or automatically transfer account ownership. Recovery requires verification. If you are completely locked out, contact TikNation Support from a safe email address and include your username. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

What we protect

  • Original photos, videos, and posts
  • Followers, following, and creator history
  • Username, profile, badges, and account identity
  • Changes to phone, email, bio, and external links
  • Unauthorized sessions and account changes

Sensitive recovery evidence is restricted. Staff do not get access to private messages or passwords as part of account recovery.
