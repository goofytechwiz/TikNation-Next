Victim Support & Safeguarding

A safe response can help someone feel heard and get connected to qualified help. Volunteers do not interrogate, investigate, or promise secrecy.

Reading lesson

1. Listen without blame

Stay calm. Thank the person for telling you. Say that the harm is not their fault. Avoid judgment, disbelief, leading questions, or asking them to repeat details unnecessarily.

2. Do not investigate or request material

Do not ask for explicit images, request screenshots of sexual material, download or forward files, confront the alleged offender, or promise to keep everything secret. Explain that you may need to share limited information with designated safeguarding staff or authorities to help protect someone.

3. Connect to appropriate help

For immediate danger in the U.S., call 911. For suspected online child sexual exploitation, use the NCMEC CyberTipline. If the person is a minor, help them reach a trusted safe adult. Follow local child-protection and mandatory-reporting rules as applicable.
