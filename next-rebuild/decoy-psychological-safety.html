Psychological Safety & Trauma Awareness

Exposure to threats, manipulation, and disturbing disclosures can affect volunteers. You are allowed to stop, pause, and ask for support.

Reading lesson

1. Recognize stress responses

Stress can show up as anxiety, anger, intrusive thoughts, trouble sleeping, numbness, irritability, or feeling compelled to keep checking messages. People respond differently; there is no single correct reaction.

2. Set limits and step away

Do not view disturbing content for training curiosity. Stop a task if you feel unsafe or overwhelmed. Tell the designated supervisor, use the program's pause or withdrawal process, and avoid discussing sensitive details in public or with people who do not need to know.

3. Support and recovery

Talk with a trusted person or qualified mental-health professional if distress persists. In the U.S., call or text 988 for crisis support. If there is immediate danger, call 911. Seeking help is responsible, not a failure.
