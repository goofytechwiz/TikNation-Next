What this first version can—and cannot—do

It can display an animated, clearly fictional adult avatar and let an approved advocate change its appearance and background for a safety-education demonstration. It cannot act as a virtual webcam in Zoom, TikNation LIVE, or another app. A true system-level virtual camera requires a separate, signed camera-driver or desktop integration and a much stricter review; this browser page does not claim to provide that.

TikNation policy still prohibits using real children or child-like personas as decoys, sexualized content, public accusations, doxxing, viewer mobilization, and livestreamed confrontations. Suspected exploitation should be reported privately to TikNation and the appropriate authorities.
