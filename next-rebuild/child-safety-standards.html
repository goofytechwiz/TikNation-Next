Child Safety Standards & Accountability

Protecting children is a shared responsibility. These standards set expectations for every TikNation member, creator, moderator, employee, contractor, advocate, and volunteer—regardless of status or role.

1 · Our commitment

TikNation does not tolerate child sexual exploitation, grooming, coercion, sexualization of minors, trafficking-related exploitation, or attempts to use the platform to harm or manipulate children. We aim to provide age-appropriate experiences, accessible reporting, privacy-conscious review, and clear accountability.

No online service can promise to detect or prevent every harmful interaction. Members should use reporting tools and seek immediate real-world help when someone is in danger.

2 · Rules that apply to everyone

  • Never create, request, solicit, possess through TikNation, upload, post, transmit, or distribute sexual images or videos of anyone under 18. Do not ask someone to resend such material as proof.
  • Never groom, sexually solicit, threaten, blackmail, exploit, traffic, or attempt to arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Never encourage a minor to keep unsafe secrets, move to private channels to evade safeguards, share intimate material, or meet an adult in secret.
  • Never use threats, gifts, money, status, job offers, creator opportunities, or emotional pressure to obtain sexual access, images, secrecy, or control.
  • Never publish a child's identifying details, school, address, live location, contact information, or private disclosures without a lawful and appropriate basis.
  • Never retaliate against someone for making a good-faith safety report or cooperating with a review.
  • Never impersonate a child, conduct a private sting, bait or contact suspected offenders, arrange meetings, confront people, doxx, threaten, or livestream accusations in the name of child safety.
Important: If sexual material involving a minor appears, do not download, save, screenshot, forward, upload, or circulate it. Stop viewing and follow official reporting instructions.

3 · Adult-to-minor boundaries

  • Adults must maintain appropriate, transparent, non-sexual boundaries with minors at all times.
  • Adults must not initiate private conversations with minors for romantic, sexual, secretive, or manipulative purposes.
  • Where a legitimate support or moderation interaction is necessary, it should use an approved platform process, be limited to the stated purpose, and be subject to appropriate oversight and recordkeeping.
  • Adults must not ask minors for personal contact details, private images, off-platform contact, secrecy, or in-person meetings.
  • Creators, moderators, staff, and volunteers must not use their role, authority, or access to form exploitative relationships with minors.
  • Members should never assume that a verified badge, staff title, creator status, or volunteer approval makes a person safe or gives them authority outside their assigned role.

These are conduct standards. Actual product controls—such as age gates, messaging permissions, and review workflows—must be configured and tested separately; this policy page alone does not technically enforce them.

4 · Responsibilities by role

All members and creators

Follow the rules, protect private information, report concerns in good faith, and do not investigate or expose people yourself.

Moderators, employees, and contractors

Use only approved tools and the minimum access needed for assigned work. Protect report confidentiality, document actions accurately, escalate urgent concerns, and never use private data for personal reasons.

Advocates and volunteers

Provide education and safe referrals only within an approved scope. No role grants police powers, investigative authority, or automatic access to private messages, child communications, identity records, or safety reports. Volunteer simulations must be fictional, adult-only, supervised, and contained within approved training environments.

Founders and designated safety reviewers

Review reports impartially, prioritize immediate safety, limit access to sensitive information, document decisions, manage conflicts of interest, and use proportionate action. A report is an allegation to assess—not proof by itself.

5 · Reporting and urgent escalation

  1. Use TikNation's in-app reporting route for concerning content, conduct, or accounts. Include factual context and relevant account or content links where safe.
  2. Do not attach or link sexual images or videos of minors, passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
  3. If a child is in immediate danger in the United States, call 911. Do not wait for a platform response.
  4. For suspected online child sexual exploitation in the United States, report through the official NCMEC CyberTipline ↗.
  5. For concerns outside the United States, contact the appropriate local emergency, child-protection, or law-enforcement service.
  6. Do not confront the reported person, attempt a rescue, arrange a meeting, or conduct your own investigation.

TikNation is not law enforcement or an emergency service. Platform reporting does not replace contacting emergency services when someone faces immediate danger.

6 · Privacy, access, and confidentiality

  • Safety information must be handled discreetly and shared only with people who have a legitimate, assigned need to know.
  • Access to sensitive reports and personal information must be role-limited. Approval as an advocate or volunteer does not grant access to private user messages, child communications, identity documents, or restricted review queues.
  • Do not copy, export, download, or discuss case information outside approved workflows. Never use report information for curiosity, retaliation, publicity, or personal contact.
  • Do not promise absolute secrecy. Explain that limited information may need to be shared with designated safety personnel or authorities to protect someone or meet legal obligations.
  • Collect and retain only information necessary for a legitimate safety purpose, in accordance with applicable law and TikNation's privacy and retention practices.

7 · Review, protective action, and fairness

TikNation may review reports and take proportionate action under its rules, which may include content removal, warnings, restrictions, suspension, or account termination. Where appropriate and lawful, serious concerns may be referred to relevant authorities.

  • Consider context and available reliable information. Do not treat an allegation as a proven fact.
  • Prioritize immediate safety and use the least restrictive action that reasonably addresses the risk, unless urgent protective action is needed.
  • Document decisions and the reason for significant restrictions.
  • Protect reporters and affected people from retaliation and unnecessary exposure.
  • Provide an appeal or review route where available and appropriate. Appeals do not guarantee reversal and may be limited where doing so could create a safety or legal risk.

This policy does not promise a specific response time or outcome for every report. Urgent real-world danger must be reported to emergency services.

8 · Conflicts of interest and misconduct

Anyone reviewing or handling a safety concern must disclose personal, financial, or other conflicts that could affect impartiality. A conflicted person should not control the review or access case details beyond what is necessary to disclose the conflict.

Misuse of authority, unauthorized access, retaliation, deliberate mishandling of reports, concealment of abuse, or violations of these standards may result in removal of permissions, suspension, termination of a role, or account action, as appropriate. Serious concerns may be escalated to relevant authorities.

9 · Training and continuous improvement

People assigned safety responsibilities must receive role-appropriate training and periodic refreshers. Training should cover boundaries, privacy, safe reporting, child-centered support, account security, escalation, and the limits of their authority. Training completion is not a professional license or investigative credential.

These standards should be reviewed periodically and updated when law, product features, safety risks, or operational procedures change. Updates should be communicated to people whose responsibilities are affected.

10 · How to get help

If you or someone else is unsafe, seek help from a trusted adult or qualified professional. For immediate danger in the United States, call 911. For online child sexual exploitation concerns, use the NCMEC CyberTipline.

This document describes TikNation's safety expectations and educational framework. It is not legal advice, a guarantee of detection or prevention, or a substitute for emergency services and official investigations.
