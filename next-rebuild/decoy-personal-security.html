Personal Security & Identity Protection

Protect your identity, accounts, location, and people close to you. A volunteer's safety comes before any program activity.

Reading lesson

1. Reduce your public footprint

Review public profile details, old posts, photos, workplace details, location tags, and friend lists. Avoid posting real-time location, routines, travel plans, or identifying details about family members. Ask trusted people not to tag your location without permission.

2. Secure accounts and devices

Use unique passwords stored in a reputable password manager, turn on multifactor authentication, keep devices and apps updated, use a screen lock, and secure the email account used for password recovery. Review active sessions and connected apps.

3. If you are doxxed or impersonated

Do not retaliate or publish the other person's information. Save safe evidence such as URLs, usernames, timestamps, and threat text; report the content to the platform; tighten privacy settings; tell the program lead; and contact local authorities if there is a credible threat or immediate danger.
