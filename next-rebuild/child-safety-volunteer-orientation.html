What you are applying to do

This program supports child-safety awareness, safer community practices, and responsible reporting. It is not an undercover unit, a private investigation service, or a substitute for trained child-protection professionals.

Absolute boundary: You must never pose as a child, create a child-like identity, bait or privately engage a suspected offender, test a real person's intentions, arrange a meeting, confront or threaten anyone, or run a sting on TikNation or another platform. Volunteers do not investigate suspects.

The three volunteer pathways

Supervised simulation participant

Take part in fictional, adult-only training scenarios inside TikNation. Practice recognizing warning signs, choosing safe responses, and escalating a concern to the designated safety team. Scenarios never involve real suspects or real children.

Real-world safety reporter

Report concerning content or conduct that you independently encounter through the platform's reporting tools or appropriate official channels. Do not initiate contact to gather proof, investigate accounts, follow people across platforms, or collect material.

Adult image contributor

May request a separate, specific review of an adult-owned image for a disclosed educational asset or clearly fictional adult simulation inside TikNation. This is optional and requires separate informed consent for each use. No image uploads or likeness use are currently enabled.

Shared expectations

Follow instructions, protect privacy, stay within your assigned role, complete refreshers, report mistakes promptly, and step away if material becomes distressing. Founder approval is not professional certification.

Recognizing concerning patterns

Harmful contact can begin with ordinary conversation and develop gradually. Warning signs can include repeated attempts to move a conversation into private or disappearing channels, secrecy requests, boundary testing, gifts or favors tied to attention, isolation from trusted adults, requests for personal details, pressure to prove trust, threats, blackmail, or requests for intimate images.

One sign alone does not prove criminal conduct. Do not label, accuse, or investigate a person. Preserve your own safety and report the concern through the appropriate channel.

Trafficking and exploitation awareness

Possible indicators can include someone else controlling a person's communication or movement, coercion, threats, debt manipulation, exploitation for money or services, or a person appearing unable to speak freely. Indicators are not proof. Never confront a suspected controller or attempt a rescue yourself.

Sextortion and image-based abuse

If someone threatens to share an intimate image or demands money, more images, or contact, do not negotiate or pay. Do not forward the image. Report the account/content and seek help from appropriate authorities or a trusted safeguarding professional. A young person should not be blamed for being manipulated.

How to respond safely

  1. Do not investigate. Stop any interaction that feels unsafe. Do not bait, test, impersonate, threaten, or arrange a meeting.
  2. Do not collect explicit material. Never request, download, screenshot, upload, forward, store, or circulate child sexual abuse material or intimate images of a minor. Do not ask a child to send an image as proof.
  3. Record only necessary non-explicit facts. If safe and permitted, note the platform, public account identifier, date/time, and a brief factual description. Follow platform instructions. Do not make copies of prohibited material.
  4. Use official reporting routes. Report through the platform and relevant child-protection or law-enforcement channels. Follow their instructions; do not submit a report on behalf of someone without permission unless a legal duty or emergency requires it.
  5. Escalate immediate danger. In the United States call 911 for immediate danger. NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org. National Human Trafficking Hotline: 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  6. Protect confidentiality. Share only with authorized safety personnel. Never post allegations, names, screenshots, or case details publicly or in creator chats.
If a child tells you something concerning: listen calmly, thank them for telling you, do not promise secrecy, do not interrogate them, do not ask for explicit details, and connect them with a trusted safeguarding professional or appropriate reporting service.

Privacy, consent, and emotional safety

  • Use only your own account and truthful adult identity. Do not borrow another person's account or likeness.
  • Never access private messages or personal data unless your specific platform role explicitly grants it. This volunteer program grants no such access.
  • Do not save case material to personal devices, cloud drives, or group chats.
  • Adult image contributors must be 18+, own or control the rights to each offered asset, and approve the exact purpose, platform, audience, duration, and presentation. Consent to use an image is not consent to impersonate the contributor or speak for them. Consent can be withdrawn for future use.
  • No intimate images, sexualized edits, childlike styling, AI sexual alterations, or external-platform deception. No images or identity documents are collected through this application.
  • Distressing content can affect sleep, mood, and wellbeing. Pause, seek support, and tell the program lead if you need to step back. You will not be pressured to continue.

Selection and what happens after applying

  1. Complete this orientation and pass the knowledge check (at least 8 of 10 correct).
  2. Submit an adult volunteer application and choose the role(s) you want to be considered for.
  3. The Founder reviews eligibility, suitability, and safeguarding readiness. Additional information or a conversation may be requested.
  4. If selected, you receive role-specific instructions and supervised onboarding. Approval is limited to the roles stated in your decision and may be suspended or withdrawn.

The orientation pass is not a background check, professional credential, law-enforcement authorization, or guarantee of acceptance. TikNation may pause or close applications while safety controls are reviewed.
